[ Case study (02) ] — UX Project · Generative-AI Hackathon

Coin Toss

Ever felt overwhelmed by travel planning? You're not alone — so flip a coin. I led user interviews and built a user-centered prototype for an AI-powered travel planner during a 10-day generative-AI hackathon.

Role
UX Researcher & Interface Designer
Tools
Figma, Illustrator, Gemini
Team & timeline
6 people · 10 days
Status
Completed

Project overview

Coin Toss is a mobile app that eases the stress of travel planning. Many travelers face decision fatigue, information overload, and the complexity of building personalized itineraries.

Coin Toss uses generative AI to create customized itineraries from a traveler's stated preferences and style — and its signature "Coin Toss" feature helps users settle on a plan in a fun, low-stakes way.

01

Understand user pain

Pain points

Indecisiveness

Groups and individuals struggle to agree on where to go and what to do.

Lack of personalization

Generic recommendations rarely match individual preferences, leading to so-so trips.

Itinerary gridlock

Even after deciding on a place, settling the day-to-day plan is hard.

Potential solutions

Limit the choices

AI offers two preference-based itineraries so the decision stays simple.

Input travel style

Travelers enter their style to generate a customized itinerary.

Coin toss

Stuck between two options? Our signature coin toss settles the debate.

02

Competitive analysis

Wanderlog

Personalized itineraries with real-time insights, but plans can feel overly packed.

Funliday

Has an AI chatbot, but users must manually transfer the info into the app.

Nowy

Great collaboration features, but lacks real-time updates and customization.

OOO

Personalized recommendations, but occasional inaccuracies in local info.

03

Design process & development

Sketch

We sketched and brainstormed to surface innovative ideas — including the "Coin Toss" decision tool. Sketching let us visualize and iterate fast, leading to a refined, user-friendly design.

Design system

We built on Material 3 — Google's widely adopted framework — as the foundational design language for consistency, fitting for a Google hackathon. We layered on a custom type guideline to match the app's brand and improve readability.

The features

Personalized prompt

Enter travel style, budget, companions, and preferences to generate a custom itinerary.

Generate 2 itineraries

AI returns two options to make the final decision easier.

Flip a coin to decide

When choices get hard, toss a coin to settle it.

Audio guide

Explore destinations with narration tailored to where you are.

Social norms & local prices

Gemini-powered tips on local customs, etiquette, and pricing.

City safety check

Crime-rate context and a safety check-in for solo travelers.

05

Reflection

This hackathon was a chance to rapidly build a user-centered app. Working within 10 days demanded efficient collaboration and focused execution, and generative AI (Gemini) was the key differentiator — powering highly personalized itineraries.

If I revisited it, I'd focus on:

More rigorous prompt engineering

Refining Gemini prompts to improve itinerary quality and relevance.

Larger usability sample

More extensive testing to surface usability gaps.

Travel-service integrations

Connecting booking, transit, and other services for a seamless flow.

The project deepened my belief in user research and iterative design — even on a short timeline — and showed AI's potential to solve real problems. Most of all, it fueled my interest in the intersection of AI and user-centered design.
