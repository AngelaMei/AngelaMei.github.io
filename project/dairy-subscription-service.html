Project overview

This project tackled a local dairy agency's biggest pain: a severe shortage of customer-service staff. We built a Line bot to automate key customer interactions, streamline stock management, and improve delivery efficiency.

01

Timeline

02

The company's challenge

A start-up promoting dairy in a competitive market — with a limited marketing budget and a specific audience that traditional advertising couldn't reach.

Lack of workforce

Just 3 staff handled 100+ customers a day, leading to constant overwork.

Stock management

Flexible delivery amounts made stock hard to forecast and balance.

Delivery optimization

The agency had to optimize routes across 600 addresses per week to cut costs.

03

Core findings

From data in the original system, qualitative interviews, and surveys, three things stood out.

78%

of customers were frustrated by changing weekly amounts and long service waits.

69%

of customer-service time went to adjusting delivery amounts.

20%

of stock was wasted weekly from untimely order adjustments.

04

Prototype 1 & user testing

Prototype 1

After talking with the CEO and doing initial research, we first built a website where customers could check remaining stock and adjust their delivery order.

User testing

When customers tried the first prototype, reactions weren't great. They found downloading and learning a new app inconvenient — which hurt accessibility and willingness to buy.

Preference for existing platforms

Customers strongly preferred Line — Taiwan's most-used messaging app.

Limited tech savviness

50% of the base were aged 50+ with limited tech proficiency.

Lack of interaction

Customers missed human contact, reducing trust and purchase intent.

05

Improvement & Prototype 2

Redesign

We'd missed something important: 92% of customers already used Line for customer service (8% used Meta or phone calls). So instead of a new website, we extended the existing Line Official Account with an automatic-response bot.

Focus on the Line platform

Designed the whole flow inside Line so users never have to leave the app.

Customer self-service

Customers adjust their delivery amount before dispatch — keeping flexibility while freeing up staff time.

Backend solutions

Efficient data management

A scalable database for customers, orders, stock levels, and delivery schedules.

Automated order processing

Order confirmation, stock adjustments, and scheduling automated to minimize manual work.

06

AI-generated illustrations

Bing AI

The company's logo features a border collie, so I used Bing AI to generate a series of charming border-collie illustrations woven throughout the Line bot. They reinforced the brand and acted as visual cues — a happy border collie accompanying the "Order Confirmation" message, for example — guiding users through the bot's features.

07

Project status

Live & working

Backstage statistics show the bot reduces manual messages by 50% per month. The solution is simpler and cheaper than a website — and more effective at solving the real problem.

08

Reflection

This project was a lesson in user-centered design and platform accessibility. The first prototype was functional but missed the audience's real needs — the pivot to Line was what made it succeed.

It reinforced the value of thorough research and iterative design. I learned to leverage AI tools for visual design and to build a scalable backend, and saw how integrating into existing, widely used platforms maximizes adoption and minimizes friction.

Next, I'd explore more personalization in the bot — like delivery recommendations based on past orders and stock — and run more rigorous A/B testing on UI and conversational flows. Above all, this project showed me how crucial UX is to a product's success, which is exactly why I'm now pursuing HCI studies.
