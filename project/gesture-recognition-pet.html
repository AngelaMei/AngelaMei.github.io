Augmented Reality · Class Project

Pat Pet

Bring joy and connection to your world with an AR pet that learns, grows, and plays by your side. I designed and built an augmented-reality companion with intuitive gesture interactions for training and play.

Role

UI/UX Designer & Developer

Tools

Figma, Unity, C#, Blender

Team

2-person AR class project

Type

Augmented Reality

Project overview

Pets offer companionship and emotional support, but owning a real one isn't always feasible. So we built an AR pet app that users can train, nurture, and play with through intuitive gestures and a friendly interface.

Our initial focus was interactive, engaging AR pets — while acknowledging the potential of emotion-recognition technology to further personalize the experience.

01

Why, who, what?

Why

Many people love animals and value the support they provide, but can't own a real pet due to restrictions or circumstances.

Who

Pet lovers facing barriers to ownership — pet-restricted housing or financial constraints that make it impractical.

What

An AR platform with an interactive virtual pet that combats loneliness and brings the everyday joy of keeping a companion.

User story

The user downloads the app, finishes a short onboarding, and picks a pet that appears in their space through the phone. The pet responds to real-world objects — playing fetch with a tennis ball — and to gestures like a virtual handshake. As the user talks to the pet, it responds with supportive expressions, and they can even walk it in the real world for an immersive, engaging experience.

02

Core functionalities

Virtual doll

Scan your own doll to bring an emotional-support object to life — deepening attachment to something familiar.

Mood tracking

Recognize facial expressions — happiness, sadness, anger, stress — so the AR pet can respond and chat via generative AI.

Gesture recognition

Engage your pet through gestures to build intimacy — with gesture teaching and mood sharing today, and feeding and walking next.

03

Challenges, breakthroughs & detours

Unable to use Unity Cloud

Collaboration obstacles led us to move the project code to GitHub.

Hand collision detection

Accurate, robust collision between hands and objects is genuinely complex.

Finger length varies

Differences in finger length affect the accuracy of detection.

Plugin conflicts

The emotion-recognition plugin clashed with some Unity settings, causing errors.

04

Design strategy

MDA framework

Guided by Mechanics–Dynamics–Aesthetics, the app was designed to cultivate deep emotional connections. Interactive elements go beyond passive engagement, fostering intimacy and trust between users and their companions — encouraging people to express emotions freely in a safe space, and providing a valuable outlet for stress relief.

05

User feedback & improvements

Onboarding coach marks

Interactive tutorials address the confusion of navigating AR, with clear instructions for interacting with AR elements.

Gesture instruction guide

An in-app guide demonstrates available gestures and rewards users for deepening the bond with their pet.

Progress bar

A dynamic indicator visualizes the growing bond, motivating consistent interaction.

Emotion diary

Users record daily emotions and receive personalized suggestions based on their entries.
