[ Case study (02) ] — iOS app · Personal project · Live on the App Store

MuPa

A digital stamp passport for the museums of Washington, D.C. I designed, illustrated and built an iOS app that lets people check in at 40+ museums with GPS and collect a hand-drawn stamp for every visit.

Role
Product Designer, Illustrator & iOS Developer
Tools
SwiftUI, MapKit, Core Location, Adobe Illustrator
Timeline
Jul – Aug 2025
Status
Live on the App Store · v1.0.4

Project overview

Washington, D.C. has dozens of museums — most of them free. That makes them easy to visit and just as easy to forget. MuPa (Museum Passport) turns each visit into a keepsake: walk in, check in, and a stamp lands in your passport.

It's built for people living in or visiting D.C. who want a light, playful way to track and celebrate their cultural journey.

01

One idea: the passport stamp

Paper passport stamps are a small, physical reward for showing up. I wanted the same feeling on a phone — so the check-in had to depend on actually being there, and the reward had to be worth collecting.

40+

museums across D.C., Maryland and Virginia, each with its own stamp.

100 m

check-in radius — you can only collect a stamp when you're at the museum.

0

accounts or tracking — visits stay on the device; the app collects no data.

02

Four tabs, one journey

The app follows a visit from start to finish: find a museum, get there, check in, look back. Each step got its own tab, with the passport first because it's the reason to open the app.

Passport

Progress at a glance — all museums and Smithsonian museums as rings — then the stamp grid, with empty frames for the ones still to collect.

Map

Every museum pinned with its category icon (art, history, science, nature, culture), so you can plan a route along the Mall.

Museums

Search, category filters and a Nearby toggle. Cards show hours, distance and whether entry is free.

Stamp Log

A timeline of every visit. Records can be added manually, edited or deleted — for the days GPS doesn't cooperate.

03

A stamp for every museum

The stamps are the reward, so I drew each one by hand: a perforated frame, a single bold symbol for the museum — a dinosaur skull, a plane, a portrait — and its name in hand-lettered type. Flat colour and a thick outline keep them readable at thumbnail size and give the set one voice.

Brand

The same hand-drawn line carries into the logo — a passport with a Capitol dome — the category icons and the tab bar, on a warm cream background instead of plain white.

04

Designed and built end to end

I built the app myself in SwiftUI, which let me tune the details directly in code: the check-in only unlocks within 100 metres of a museum (Core Location), the map uses MapKit with custom pins, and all visits are stored locally on the device.

05

Outcome

MuPa is live on the App Store as a free iPhone app, rated 5.0 by its first reviewers. Next on the list: photos on each visit, sharing a finished passport, and seasonal stamps for special exhibitions.
