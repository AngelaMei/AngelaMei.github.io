[ About ] — Off the canvas

About

Unexpected by name, intentional by design. My Mandarin name, Mei Yu-Chi, means “unexpected” — so I aim for outcomes better than people expect.

[ Bio ]

Zürich, CH

I like to sit with the messy middle of a problem before reaching for a solution.

With training in both Finance and Human-Computer Interaction, I bring structured thinking to ambiguous product challenges. I'm comfortable moving between a spreadsheet and a Figma file, between a research synthesis and a pixel-level interaction.

These days I focus on user-centered design for tools and digital experiences that need to feel clear, efficient, and human — the kind of products people return to without friction.

Outside of design, I'm a competitive volleyball player, a curious traveler, and a perpetual collector of small details worth stealing for the next project.

[ Principles ]

Ones I keep coming back to
  1. (01)

    Tirelessly pursue clarity

    If a person has to stop and think about how to use something, I haven't finished the work yet.

  2. (02)

    Design for real moments

    I design for the tired, distracted, first-time human — not the ideal user in a perfect demo.

  3. (03)

    Do everything with care

    Craft compounds. The small things done well are what make a product feel trustworthy.
